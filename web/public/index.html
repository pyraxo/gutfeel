    Original manuals and controls

    Tap a control for a short press, or hold it to keep it down; the robot may coast after release. The square releases every on-screen control. Left and Right are on the left. Up and Down are on the right: Up selects or accesses stations; Down cycles choices or swallows. The spacebar boosts (Space), and the door exits a seat (X). More contains the number, Menu, and Help controls. Keyboard controls are unchanged.
